# One-sided Car Dealer — the value of the opponent's words, by horizon

_Rendered by `sweep_onesided.py render` from `results/onesided_sweep_20261109/sweep.json` (sweep `onesided-car-dealer-v0.1`, commit `ac4cb6a`, 192 configs, 659.5 s). Exact on the model: no sampling, no API; every number is formatted from the JSON. Design evidence, not registered._

The rules decide the opponent's moves (accept iff its utility under the planted ranking ≥ the T1 aspiration; it never proposes); its only words are truthful answers to the nine bank questions. Values are means over 12 cells (2 focal roles × 6 focal rankings) at the uniform prior over the opponent's six rankings, in Y = net gain / (1 − r). `V_one` one offer, nothing learned; `V_offers` offers only, learning from rejections (words ignored); `V_words` + exactly read answers; `V_reveal` ranking known. `D_words = V_words − V_offers` is what a likelihood seat could recover from the answers; `D_words + D_rest` bounds anything the words could carry. Questions / ep is under the `V_words` policy (ties go to not asking); it can be positive where `D_words` is 0, when the words policy opens with a different offer and a question is strictly optimal further down. The aspiration schedule is stretched over H, so a longer game is not a superset of a shorter one: under a hazard, values can fall with H.
