# Experiment A — Pilot + Determinism Audit (goal × history, 10 seeds)

**Status:** Pilot (n = 10 matched seeds × 4 conditions = 40 episodes) **re-run once
as a determinism audit.** The audit did **not** reproduce the pilot's headline —
see below. **No conclusion about an interaction is supported.**
**Model:** `jev-latest` · **Env:** real TextWorld Text-Nav, PO, 20-step limit.
**Date:** 2026-10-25.

## Bottom line

A single run of this pilot suggested a goal×history interaction (A4 winning
3/10). **An identical re-run produced A4 0/10.** `jev-latest`'s per-episode
trajectories are substantially stochastic — only **8 of 40** (seed, condition)
command sequences were byte-identical across the two runs — and all three of the
pilot's A4 wins evaporated on re-run. The point estimate was noise around a
threshold, not a stable capability. **One pass per seed is insufficient; success
must be estimated as a rate over repeated rollouts.**

## What actually holds (stable across both runs)

1. **A1/A2/A3 robustly fail at or before M1.** Their outcomes were identical
   across runs despite different command sequences — the trajectory noise wanders
   among equally unproductive actions and reaches the same (lack of) progress.
   None ever passes M1.
2. **A4 is the only condition that ever composes beyond M1** (reaches M2+ in both
   runs: 4 seeds in the pilot, 1 in the audit). So "explicit goal + history" does
   appear to be the only setting with any chance of sequential composition — but
   whether an episode *completes* is highly run-dependent and was **0/10 on
   re-run**, so no success rate can be claimed from this data.
3. **Eating stays fully suppressed** — 0 eats across 350 (pilot) / 372 (audit)
   opportunities, in every condition. This replicates and is robust.

## What does NOT hold

- **The goal×history interaction / "A4 wins 3/10".** Not reproducible. A single
  run overstated a threshold-crossing that re-running erased.
- **Any per-condition success rate.** With 1 rollout/seed and stochastic
  trajectories, binary success is a coin flip at the margins, not a measurement.

## Correction to the determinism assumption

The earlier 12-call determinism probe concluded the argmax was deterministic. That
was true **only for the wide-margin states it happened to test** (0.99 vs 0.01;
0.75 vs 0.21). Real episodes contain many near-tie decisions where the argmax
flips between runs, so **realized trajectories are not deterministic** and 1
pass/seed cannot estimate success. Repeats are mandatory. (Design Appendix E
updated accordingly.)

## Required next steps (before any main run)

1. **Repeats calibration.** For a few seeds (e.g. the three where A4 crossed —
   1, 3, 4 — plus a couple of trapped seeds), run K independent rollouts per
   condition (K ≈ 10–20) and estimate the per-(seed, condition) success
   *probability* and its spread. This tells us (a) whether A4's completions are a
   real, repeatable rate (> 0) that A1–A3 lack, or flukes, and (b) the K needed
   for the main run. The runner now supports `--repeats K`.
2. **Only then, the main run.** 50 seeds × 4 conditions × K rollouts, reporting
   per-seed success rates with cluster-bootstrap CIs — not single-pass binary
   outcomes. Pre-register the contrasts first.
